================================================================================
                            WORD MOTIONS CHEATSHEET
================================================================================
w        - Jump to start of next word
b        - Jump to start of previous word
e        - Jump to end of current/next word
ge       - Jump to end of previous word
W        - Jump to start of next WORD (whitespace-separated)
B        - Jump to start of previous WORD
E        - Jump to end of current/next WORD
gE       - Jump to end of previous WORD

{count}w - Jump forward {count} words (e.g., 5w)
{count}b - Jump backward {count} words (e.g., 3b)

Word = sequence of letters, digits, underscores OR sequence of other characters
WORD = sequence of non-blank characters (separated by whitespace)
